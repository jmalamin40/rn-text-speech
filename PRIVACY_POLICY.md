# Privacy Policy for TextToSpeech BD

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2025

## Introduction

TextToSpeech BD ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App").

## Information We Collect

### Text Input
- **What we collect:** The text you enter into the app to convert to speech
- **How we use it:** We send your text to our text-to-speech service (texttospeechfree.online) to generate audio
- **Storage:** Your text is processed in real-time and is NOT stored on our servers
- **Retention:** Text is processed and immediately discarded after audio generation

### Audio Files
- **What we collect:** Generated audio files are temporarily stored on your device
- **How we use it:** Audio files are saved locally for playback and download functionality
- **Storage:** Audio files are stored only on your device, not on our servers
- **Retention:** You can delete audio files at any time from your device

### Device Information
- **What we collect:** Standard device information (device model, OS version) for app functionality
- **How we use it:** Used to ensure app compatibility and improve user experience
- **Storage:** Information is not stored or transmitted to third parties

## Third-Party Services

### Text-to-Speech API
- We use the texttospeechfree.online API to convert text to speech
- Your text is sent to this service for processing
- Please refer to their privacy policy for their data handling practices

## Permissions

### Internet Permission
- **Required for:** Connecting to the text-to-speech API
- **Usage:** Only used to send text and receive audio files

### Storage Permission (Android 10-12 only)
- **Required for:** Saving downloaded audio files to your device
- **Usage:** Only used when you choose to download audio files
- **Note:** Not required on Android 13+ due to scoped storage

## Data Security

- We use HTTPS encryption for all network communications
- Your text is transmitted securely to the API
- No data is stored on our servers
- All processing happens in real-time

## Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

## Your Rights

- **Access:** You can view all data stored on your device
- **Deletion:** You can delete audio files from your device at any time
- **Control:** You control what text you enter and what audio files you keep

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your data to third parties
- Share your text with advertisers
- Track your usage for marketing purposes
- Store your personal information

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
